This runs a JavaScript reimplementation of our controller's decision rule — the same minimum/maximum green times, queue+wait priority score, and beacon-preemption logic as the Python/TraCI version — simplified for instant, in-browser use rather than running full SUMO. Emergency vehicles can pass queued cars on both sides, as they would on a two-lane road; what differs is whether the signal turns green for them. The adaptive controller gets about 10 seconds of beacon warning before an emergency vehicle arrives, standing in for the 150 m detection range in our SUMO setup.
